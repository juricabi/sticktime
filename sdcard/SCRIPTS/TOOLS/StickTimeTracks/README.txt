Your own tracks: c1.txt to c8.txt are tracks 9 to 16 in StickTime and
StickTime BW (StickTime Lite does not read them). A bigger track (more than
24 gates, 16 structures or 999 bytes) needs StickTime 1.8.0 or later on a
color radio. Make them with the track
editor in the browser emulator, https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/
(Tracks): Save file and copy it here, or, in Chrome or Edge on a computer
with the radio in USB Storage mode, Send to radio. Restart StickTime on
the radio to see a new track in its Track list.
