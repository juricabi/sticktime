StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.5-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.5
- StickTime BW and Lite start on color radios too (B&W style at full resolution).
- Ground effect near the ground and roofs.

### New in 1.4
- Grippier quads (a slide halves in about 2 s), max rates in 10°/s steps, and Show FPS reports the game's and the screen's time on the faster firmware.

All releases: [https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).
