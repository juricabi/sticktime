StickTime 1.6.5 (stock flight model) keeps the 1.6.4 flight model and adds the browser emulator's sound and music.

### New in 1.6.5 (stock flight model)
- **Motor sound in the browser emulator** (the radio still can't do better than a plain tone): four synthesized motors, each with its own rpm that follows your stick inputs, tuned against recordings of real quads - a nearly pure blade-pass tone over a soft air whoosh (no hiss), shaft growl and electrical whine, a steady pitch, panned left and right, beating like real props; a dark rumble descending into your own wash, an air-rush voice that grows with speed, a thud on crashes, a swell on spool-up. The **Sound** slider (0 is off) now sets the beeps' volume too, on a loudness curve, with a mute button and mouse-wheel steps; the keyboard / gamepad choice is remembered.
- **Music in the browser emulator**: melodic electronic music composed live (free, nothing to download): every song has its own key, tempo, seventh-chord progression and hook, arranged like a real track with a filtered intro, build, drop and breakdown, or **Songs** to play your own files; its own switch, level and next-song button.
- The **Bando**'s tower is taller (36 m): room for orbits, reverse orbits and dives.
