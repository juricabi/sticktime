StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.6.5-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the whole `StickTimeBW` / `StickTimeLite` folder.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.6.5
- **A new flight model with five quads:** Racer 5", Freestyle 5", 3" toothpick, Whoop 65mm and Whoop 75mm, each fitted to a detailed per-prop model ([how](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/blob/main/docs/flight-model.md)). **Power** gains **Stock**.
- Translational lift, a descent cushion, ground effect and motor spool-up; a new **Grip** setting (rotor drag Low, Medium, High).
- **Prop wash** Off, Stock or Max, with shake measured from real flight logs.
- Browser emulator: synthesized **motor sound** and generated **music** (or your own songs).
- The Bando's tower is 36 m tall.

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
