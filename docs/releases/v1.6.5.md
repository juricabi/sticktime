StickTime is a real 3D FPV quad simulator that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.6.5-sdcard.zip** below and copy the contents of its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` folder to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the radio's SD card, then start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`
- **B&W radios with an STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019, X7 ACCESS, T20...): `StickTimeBW.lua` and the `StickTimeBW` folder
- **Older B&W radios (STM32F2:** X7, X9D, X9D+, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua` and the `StickTimeLite` folder

The B&W versions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer (2.11 is the last EdgeTX for STM32F2 radios). The color version also runs on older EdgeTX.

> The radio keeps transmitting your sticks while the sim runs: unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first.

**Try it first in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.6.5
- **Flight model 2**, an rpm-curve model with eleven presets: 5" Freestyle (680 g, 8.1:1), 5" Light, 5" Heavy, 5" Chonker, 5" Racer (480 g, 12.5:1), 5" Beginner (4.4:1), 3.5" Quad, 2.5" Analog and HD, 85mm and 65mm Whoops. Thrust straight from the motors' rpm through a steep low-rpm curve (the quads hover at 17-36% stick), less of it climbing, quadratic drag that grows with throttle (short slides: 1-2 s half-life), motors at 170 000 rpm/s pushed up by hard flips and snaps, attitude that follows the sticks almost at once. See [docs/flight-model.md](../flight-model.md). The **Power** setting gains **Stock** (the preset's own thrust/weight) and offers each preset the range that suits it (a Power the new quad doesn't have goes back to Stock).
- No descent cushion, translational lift, ground effect or frame lift: the model is simple on purpose.
- **Prop wash** Off / **Stock** / **Max**: Off is the model alone; Stock and Max add flight model 1's prop wash from the class of the same size (it costs thrust when you sink through your own downwash, and shakes the quad by tenths of a degree at 20-45 Hz, worst with the throttle chopped; edgewise speed clears it).
- **Motor sound in the browser emulator** (the radio still can't do better than a plain tone): four synthesized motors, each with its own rpm that follows your stick inputs, tuned against recordings of real quads - a nearly pure blade-pass tone over a soft air whoosh (no hiss), shaft growl and electrical whine (the whoop screams at 4-6.6 kHz), a steady pitch, panned left and right, beating like real props; a dark rumble descending into your own wash, the motors' chatter in prop wash, an air-rush voice that grows with speed, a thud on crashes, a swell on spool-up, shaped per class. The **Sound** slider (0 is off) now sets the beeps' volume too, on a loudness curve, with a mute button and mouse-wheel steps; the keyboard / gamepad choice is remembered.
- **Music in the browser emulator**: melodic electronic music composed live (free, nothing to download): every song has its own key, tempo, seventh-chord progression and hook, arranged like a real track with a filtered intro, build, drop and breakdown, or **Songs** to play your own files; its own switch, level and next-song button.
- The **Bando**'s tower is taller (36 m): room for orbits, reverse orbits and dives.
- The tests find the hover throttle from the game's own thrust curve.
- For developers: `tools/tune_physics.py` checks every preset; the headless tests cover every preset's Power range and the three prop-wash settings. The Lite reads the preset in use from `StickTimeLite/q<n>.txt` and fits STM32F2 radios on EdgeTX 2.11.

### New in 1.6.4
- StickTime BW and Lite no longer risk a crash at start. Their precompiled game is now `game.luac`, and the loader loads only that. It used to be `core.luac` next to its source `core.lua`, and EdgeTX compiles `core.lua` on the radio whenever that file looks newer, which a copy that does not keep file times can cause. A B&W radio does not have the memory to compile the game (it takes about 200 KB), and running out of it could crash the radio. **When updating, replace the whole `StickTimeBW` / `StickTimeLite` folder.**
- When the game can't start, the loader says what to do on a screen of its own: **Needs EdgeTX 2.11 or newer** on EdgeTX 2.10 and older, **Not enough memory on this radio. Use StickTime Lite.** for StickTime BW on an STM32F2 radio, or **Copy the whole folder** when the folder is missing. EXIT (RTN) closes it. EdgeTX's own error box showed a 128×64 radio only the end of these messages.
- For developers: `tools/build_etxhost.sh v2.10.7` builds EdgeTX 2.10's Lua for the memory tests, and the tests check the loader's screen on it.

### New in 1.6.3
- The end screen (race finished, Gate Rush time up) works with the radio's keys: the wheel or the page keys move between **Again** and **Menu**, and ENTER takes the one picked. Before, ENTER always started over and only EXIT led to the menu. Color, B&W and Lite.

### New in 1.6.2
- New track, **Pro Track**, laid out like a real race instead of a loop: lanes up and down the field joined by flag hairpins, a high gate and a dive gate on the start straight, a split-S (through the high gate, then back through the gate under it), hoops and a corner flag before the finish.
- The Slalom's flags make you weave now: each one is passed on its outer side, so flying straight down the middle no longer counts. The Grand Prix's two flags work the same way. Your old lap and race bests on these two tracks were flown on the old layout and start over.
- A small arrow on the OSD shows where the wind blows (up is where your nose points) when **Wind** is on.
- 6:1 is the default power (it was 5:1). A saved setting stays as it is.
- Stick view: on EdgeTX 2.12 and newer the sticks showed in the wrong boxes (mode 2 drawn as mode 3). Throttle and yaw are on the left in mode 2 again.

### New in 1.6.1
- Rates in Betaflight's own steps: expo in 0.01 steps up to 1.00, max rate up to 2000 deg/s, center from 10 deg/s, so Betaflight Actual rates go in exactly. Held keys repeat, and quick changes jump five times further.
- StickTime BW and Lite on color radios draw the camera ahead for the color screen's display delay, like the color game, so they feel as connected to the sticks.
- The motor sound from 1.6 is gone, with its setting: a Lua script on a radio can only play a plain tone or short clips one after another, and neither sounded like a quad. The beeps and the **Vibration** setting stay.

### New in 1.6
- Motor sound: a tone whose pitch follows the motors' speed, rising with the throttle and in fast flips and rolls. **Motor sound** setting: Off, Low, Mid (default) or High.
- **Vibration** setting: turn off the buzz on crashes and laps.
- Browser emulator: the gamepad axis pickers show the whole axis name.
- Faster firmware (the `fast-lua-20ms` prerelease of juricabi/edgetx): now built on current EdgeTX main.

### New in 1.5
- StickTime BW and StickTime Lite now start on color radios, drawn in B&W style at the screen's full resolution (they failed to start in 1.4).
- Ground effect: near the ground or a roof the props get up to 12% more thrust, fading out by about 1 m and with speed.

### New in 1.4
- Grippier quads: rotor drag as on a real 5" quad, so a sideways slide halves in about 2 s (it took 3.5–3.8 s).
- Max rates step by 10 deg/s, as in Betaflight.
- With **Show FPS** on, the faster firmware also reports game and screen time per frame.

**Faster firmware for color radios** (optional test builds): Lua tools run every 20 ms instead of 50 ms with much faster drawing, and every STM32H750 radio (TX16S MK3, TX15, GX15, T15 Pro, HelloRadioSky V12 and V15, and more) runs the Lua interpreter from ITCM. One zip with every color radio, flash the file named after yours: [fast-lua-20ms release](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
