StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.9.0-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.9.0
- **Colors** (color): **Settings → Colors** has natural looks: **Summer**, **Evening** and **Overcast**. The sky fades from the haze at the horizon to deep blue (or grey) higher up, the grass is duller and fades into the haze far away, and the colors are calmer: the next gate is the brightest thing in view, the other gates a light grey. Easier on the eyes, above all in FPV goggles. **Classic** is the look so far, and **Auto** (the default) keeps it on a radio. The natural looks draw a little more: on a TX16S about 4–5 ms a frame in the Bando.
- **The horizon's haze** (color, every look): no more dotted gaps in it when you roll.
- **[StickTime Pi](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime-pi)** (0.2.4 and later): on its analog video output to goggles, **Auto** is Summer and the text has a black outline, like Betaflight's OSD, readable over sky and grass alike. Motion is smoother at 60 Hz: the game steps exactly with the Pi's frames.

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
