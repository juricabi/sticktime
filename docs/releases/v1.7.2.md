StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.7.2-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.7.2
- **The next gate's marker shows where to come in from** (color): green anywhere on a gate's way-in side (within 70° of its axis), whichever way you head, or when you're heading straight into it from a steeper angle; yellow means go round. Flags: green before them, on or heading to the side you pass. Dive gates: green anywhere above them, a shallow swoop too. It goes green much earlier than in 1.7.1 (gates over 83% of the way in, flags over 80%).

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
