StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.8.0-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.8.0
- **Bigger tracks of your own** (color): up to 48 gates and 64 structures, instead of 24 and 16. The emulator's track editor saves a track in the bigger format only when it doesn't fit the regular one. StickTime BW and versions before 1.8.0 don't list bigger tracks. Many structures in view at once slow a radio down, so check **Radio load** in the emulator.
- **Structures drawn in the right order** (color): on tracks with many of them, like a building's floors and columns, far structures no longer show through nearer ones.
- **Raised gates in the right order too** (color): a dive gate or hoop high up (for example, on a roof) and a raised arch are drawn in the right order with the structures around them.
- **Track gallery:** the **Stonehenge Bando**: the unfinished Pevec centre at Lepirac in Bjelovar, Croatia, at its real size. Fly down through its open atrium, between its floors, along the wall and through the beam frames. It's a bigger track: color radios, StickTime 1.8.0 and later.
- **Emulator:** **Send to radio** now warns you when a bigger track won't show on that radio (StickTime older than 1.8.0, or no color StickTime). **Install** updates the game.

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
