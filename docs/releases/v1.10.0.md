StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.10.0-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

If you like it, you can buy me a coffee on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/juricabi).

### New in 1.10.0
- **Scenery** (color and BW): props (conifers, leafy trees, bushes, hay bales, tyre stacks, traffic cones, light masts, tents, windsocks and posts), rails between two points (fences, safety nets, race tape, power lines on pylons and banners), decals on the ground (dirt roads, tarmac, gravel, painted lines, ponds and sand), and structures in wood, brick, dark steel, white, yellow and green. Color radios draw them filled and shaded, B&W radios in lines.
- **The eight tracks have their scenery:** Meadow's hay bales, farm track and pond; Figure 8 as a car park ringed by light masts; Dive Tower's gravel and a power line; the Slalom's tyre stacks and banners; a pond in Hoop Forest's clearing; the Grand Prix's tarmac start straight with tents and banners; the Bando's tyres, mud and a power line; the Pro Track's nets, tents and masts. The gates are where they were: your best times stay.
- **A pilots' area** on every track, yours too: a tent and a **windsock** by the start pad, out of the racing line's way. The windsock hangs limp without wind and stands out downwind in a strong one, fluttering with the gusts (on the Lite as well).
- **Hits:** fences, nets and banners stop the quad, a power line's wire or race tape is a prop strike, and landing in a pond is a **SPLASH** (back at the last gate). In Freestyle, flying beneath a power line or a banner is a new trick: **UNDER**.
- **Night** (color): a new look in **Settings → Colors**: a dark sky full of stars, the moon, and gates lit like LED race gates, the next one amber.
- **Sky detail** (color, **Settings → Sky detail**, On by default): clouds drifting slowly round the sky, a forest's edge along the horizon, the sun high up, or low and orange in the Evening. It costs about 3 ms a frame on a TX16S: on a slow radio, or with a busy track of your own, turn it off.
- **The horizon** (BW and Lite): a ridge of mountains, and the start pad on the ground.
- **Your own tracks with scenery:** the emulator's track editor places props, rails and decals (or a pilots' area, a taped lane or a fence round the track at once), and **Check** says when one is in the racing line's way. A track with scenery is saved as format 3: up to 24 gates, 16 structures, 32 props, 12 rails and 8 decals, for color and B&W radios. A bigger one is format 4 (up to 48 gates, 64 structures, 128 props, 32 rails and 32 decals), for color radios. Both need StickTime 1.10.0; earlier versions don't list them, and tracks without scenery are saved as before.
- **Track gallery:** five tracks to show it off: **Farm Field**, **Power Line**, **Parking Lot** (fly it at Night), **Lakeside** and the **Container Yard** (a bigger track: color radios).

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
