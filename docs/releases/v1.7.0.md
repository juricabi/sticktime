StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.7.0-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.7.0
- **Hits don't end the flight:** the quad stops and slides on; a hard edge-on hit is a prop strike that tumbles it; on its back it's **turtle mode** (roll or pitch to flip over). New **Respawn** in the pause menu.
- **Medals:** Gold, Silver and Bronze are the Hard, Medium and Easy AI's lap times at your Power.
- **Battery** (Off, Real, 1.5x, 2x): each quad's own pack sags and runs down, with OSD warnings.
- **Ghost** (color): your best practice lap flies along with you.
- **New layouts** for Meadow, Dive Tower, Hoop Forest and Grand Prix (their bests start over).
- **Your own tracks** 9 to 16, made in the browser's new **track editor** and sent to the radio (StickTime and StickTime BW).
- **Install / update from the browser** (Chrome or Edge, radio in USB Storage mode).
- Bests and ghosts save the moment you set them. More memory headroom for StickTime BW. The Lite gets the new layouts only.

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
