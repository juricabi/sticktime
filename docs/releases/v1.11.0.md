StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.11.0-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

### New in 1.11.0
- **Detail** (color, **Settings → Detail**, High by default): **Low** draws the same picture simpler, for a slow radio or a busy track of your own: every other line of the fine ground grid, far hoops and gates simpler sooner, a shorter fade at the horizon, trees and bushes without their lit side, and nothing of the mountains that the haze covers. About 7% off a Classic frame and 10% off a Summer one on a TX16S. High is the picture you know, pixel for pixel; the game itself is the same at either.
- **Quicker drawing** (all editions): the same picture, pixel for pixel on the radios and on StickTime Pi, in less time: about 4% less a frame on a TX16S and 6.5% less on B&W radios.
- **Camera tilt** goes up to 90 degrees, in 5 degree steps (the Lite: 60, 70, 80 and 90 added).

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing, now on EdgeTX `main` of 25 October with quicker lines and triangles: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
