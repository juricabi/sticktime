StickTime is a 3D FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio. Download **StickTime-1.10.1-sdcard.zip** below, copy what your radio needs from its `SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` to `/SCRIPTS/TOOLS/` on the SD card and start it from **SYS → Tools**:

- **Color radios:** `StickTime.lua`, `StickTimeGhosts/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **B&W radios, STM32F4** (TX12 MkII, Zorro, Boxer, Pocket, X9D+ 2019...): `StickTimeBW.lua`, `StickTimeBW/`, `StickTimeTracks/`
- **Older B&W radios, STM32F2** (X7, X9D, X-Lite, TX12 MkI...): `StickTimeLite.lua`, `StickTimeLite/`

The B&W editions need EdgeTX 2.11 or newer. **Updating:** replace the `StickTimeBW` and `StickTimeLite` folders (keep the Lite's `data.txt`).

**Or install it from the browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/ → **Install** (Chrome or Edge, the radio in USB Storage mode). It keeps your saves, tracks and ghosts.

Unplug the quad's battery or switch the RF module off first: the radio keeps transmitting while you fly. **Try it in your browser:** https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/

If you like it, you can buy me a coffee on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/juricabi).

### New in 1.10.1
- **Lighter built-in tracks** (color): the eight tracks keep their scenery, with fewer props and shorter strips where they cost the most: fewer trees and bushes in Hoop Forest, fewer hay bales on the Meadow, two banners and one worn lane on the Slalom, shorter dirt roads and power lines. About 4% less time a frame on average, up to 8% on the Slalom. The gates are where they were: your best times stay.
- **Fly under the tents:** the pilots' tent is open on its sides, as it looks: its four legs and the canopy stop the quad, under the canopy you fly through, and on top of it you can land.
- **Power line pylons up close:** their legs and bracing stay as thick as they are when you fly right by or under one (they turned into thin lines).
- Painted lines far off are drawn as lines: quicker, and they look the same.

### New in 1.10.0
- **Scenery** (color): props (conifers, leafy trees, bushes, hay bales, tyre stacks, traffic cones, light masts, tents, windsocks and posts), rails between two points (fences, safety nets, race tape, power lines on pylons and banners), decals on the ground (dirt roads, tarmac, gravel, painted lines, ponds and sand), and structures in wood, brick, dark steel, white, yellow and green, drawn filled and shaded. B&W radios have no memory for it.
- **The eight tracks have their scenery** (color): Meadow's hay bales, farm track and pond; Figure 8 as a car park ringed by light masts; Dive Tower's gravel and a power line; the Slalom's tyre stacks and banners; a pond in Hoop Forest's clearing; the Grand Prix's tarmac start straight with tents and banners; the Bando's tyres, mud and a power line; the Pro Track's nets, tents and masts. The gates are where they were: your best times stay.
- **A pilots' area** on every track, yours too (color): a tent and a **windsock** by the start pad, out of the racing line's way. The windsock hangs limp without wind and stands out downwind in a strong one, fluttering with the gusts. B&W radios and the Lite have a windsock too, drawn in lines behind the pad.
- **Hits** (color): fences, nets, banners, race tape and a power line's wires stop the quad like a wall (a hard hit edge-on is a prop strike, as on a wall), even at full speed, and landing in a pond is a **SPLASH** (back at the last gate; in Freestyle at the start pad). In Freestyle, flying beneath a power line or a banner is a new trick: **UNDER**.
- **Night** (color): a new look in **Settings → Colors**: a dark sky full of stars, the moon, and gates lit like LED race gates, the next one amber.
- **Sky detail** (color, **Settings → Sky detail**, On by default): clouds drifting slowly round the sky, the sun high up, or low and orange in the Evening, and the moon and stars at Night. It costs under 1 ms a frame on a TX16S: on a slow radio, or with a busy track of your own, turn it off.
- **The horizon** (BW): a ridge of mountains. **The start pad** on the ground (BW and Lite). Trees keep their crowns out to 90 m, not just the near ones (BW and Lite).
- **The ground grid reaches farther:** on B&W radios the 10 m grid now runs out to 60 m as on color ones, and when you fly high a coarser 30 m grid goes on beyond it, out to 16 times your height, so it no longer ends close below you (all editions). On color radios it runs on over tarmac, dirt, gravel, sand and ponds too, in their own shades.
- **Quicker drawing** (all editions): the same picture with about 17% fewer Lua instructions a frame on color radios and 16% on B&W ones, which pays for much of the scenery.
- **Your own tracks with scenery:** the emulator's track editor places props, rails and decals (or a pilots' area, a taped lane or a fence round the track at once), and **Check** says when one is in the racing line's way. A track with scenery is saved as format 3: up to 24 gates, 16 structures, 32 props, 12 rails and 8 decals; StickTime BW flies its gates and structures. A bigger one is format 4 (up to 48 gates, 64 structures, 128 props, 32 rails and 32 decals), for color radios. Both need StickTime 1.10.0; earlier versions don't list them, and tracks without scenery are saved as before.
- **Track gallery:** five tracks to show it off: **Farm Field**, **Power Line**, **Parking Lot** (fly it at Night), **Lakeside** and the **Container Yard** (a bigger track: color radios).
- **[StickTime Pi](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime-pi)** (0.2.11 and later): its Tracks page lists the tracks with scenery, and its guard for track slots knows them. Update StickTime Pi first, then the game.

Earlier changes: [all releases](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime/releases).

**Faster firmware** (optional, color radios): Lua tools every 20 ms instead of 50, much faster drawing: [fast-lua-20ms](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms). Back up your SD card first.
